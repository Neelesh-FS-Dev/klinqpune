# Design direction

Written for: the Codevirel team reviewing the concept.

## Chosen direction
- **Main narrative: After Hours.** Midnight emerald, warm amber light, serif display type, the dining-to-night progression.
- **Flagship: The Art of the Pour.** A pinned sequence of four real drink photographs. There is no 3D glass (see `3D_ASSET_PIPELINE.md`).
- **Human layer: The Social Edition.** Guest photographs, events and celebrations, in a horizontal strip and a gallery.

## Why
- The supplied media is strongest on warm interiors, rooftop evenings, plated food and people. The three directions map onto those clearly.
- Warm emerald plus cognac reads as KLINQ's own palette (the Instagram posts use green and amber). It avoids generic black-and-gold.
- Serif display with a plain sans for UI gives editorial hierarchy without many fonts.
- The site is not a restaurant template: no card grids, no rounded service tiles, no floating shapes or particles.
