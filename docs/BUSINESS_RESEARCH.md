## 3. Editorial (third-party)
Urbanly (article first published Nov 2024, updated Apr 2025): describes a boho interior with wood and cane furniture, greenery, a fountain, and indoor and outdoor seating. It also calls the venue "newest" and mentions a skyline view. The article is over a year old, so the site does not repeat "newest" or the skyline claim.

## 4. Observed visual evidence
Supplied media (88 photos, 28 videos) show: cocktails and glassware, shared plates, rooftop evenings with lanterns, wood and botanical interiors, a neon KLINQ sign, live-music nights, and guests. Several frames carry overlaid promotional text. No pouring footage was supplied (see `3D_ASSET_PIPELINE.md`).
